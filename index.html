About Pinnacles Resource Centre Farm

Welcome to Pinnacles Resource Centre Farm — a passionate agricultural enterprise dedicated to growing and delivering the highest quality, freshest farm produce directly to your table.

We believe that good food starts with good farming. From our rich soil, we cultivate a wide range of crops including juicy tomatoes, vibrant peppers, sweet strawberries, golden maize, crunchy carrots, healthy eggs and many more seasonal crops.
